# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary audience: Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Fredrick for full-time roles. They are visiting to assess skills, view projects, and determine fit for engineering positions.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio website for Fredrick Momanyi Morara. It exists to showcase his work, skills, and experience, with the goal of securing full-time employment.

## Positioning
Full-stack developer with AI expertise. The portfolio highlights this intersection of full-stack engineering and artificial intelligence capabilities.

## Operating Context
Visitors are typically reviewing the site on desktop or mobile devices during a hiring process, often cross-referencing with a CV/resume, LinkedIn, or GitHub profile.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must remain a Next.js / React application.
- UI/UX and copywriting are areas for improvement, but the underlying tech stack is locked.

## Brand Commitments
- Current theme, colors, fonts, and tone must be preserved. The user explicitly loves the current theme.

## Evidence on Hand
- CV/Resume (to be updated by the user, currently available in PDF form).
- AI project documentation (`AI MINI TEAM PROJECT.docx.pdf`).
- These documents must be accessible to visitors.

## Product Principles
- **Clarity for Recruiters:** Information must be easily scannable and digestible for hiring managers.
- **Highlight Expertise:** The intersection of full-stack engineering and AI should be front and center.
- **Preserve Identity:** Respect and preserve the existing visual theme and brand commitments while elevating the polish.
